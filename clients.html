Key decisions: the top three suggested

  1. Start the shadow pilot

    Send the weekly briefing to Steering Committee members and a few trusted readers for three to four weeks, with Ian Collins as the named reviewer. Nothing is posted publicly before January 2027.

    To agree: the start date and the reader list.

  2. Pricing and trial

    Two paid tiers: £10 a month online (briefing and online events) and £20 a month adding in-person events. Email-first sign-up: four free issues, the price shown from the start, no automatic charge, and direct debit (GoCardless) only after an explicit choice.

    To agree: the annual price and whether AI training sits in the £20 tier.

  3. Supplier terms

    A low entry cost for the build and running of the pilot, plus a share of membership income that rises as membership passes 100 and 200 paying members.

    To agree: the entry figure and the share at each level.
