How this was built

Sources: Open Library works and editions API, the English Wikipedia bibliography, Wikidata authority IDs, the Deutsche Nationalbibliothek and the Bibliothèque nationale de France catalogues. Every ISBN was checked by checksum, and ISBN-10s were converted to ISBN-13.

    Lifespan check. Born 18 June 1946, died 21 June 2010. Editions dated 2010 or later are flagged. Top 10 of Everything editions after 2011 credit Caroline Ash (2012–13) or Paul Terry (2015 onwards) and are not counted as his.

    Availability. These are AbeBooks UK listing counts (any condition) for one or two key ISBNs per work, checked on 27 October 2026. AbeBooks throttled the follow-up new-versus-used check, so that split is shown only where it completed. New copies were also confirmed on Amazon UK for Potty, Fartwell & Knob and Bizarre Books. Ebooks come from Kindle UK and Google Play, which sells EPUB. Kobo was behind a bot check and was not checked. Prices are indicative, in GBP, before shipping, and will change.
