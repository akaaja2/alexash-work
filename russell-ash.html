You couldn't make it up. Russell Ash didn't.

Russell Ash spent a career collecting things that really exist: real names, real places and real double meanings. These books are the funniest of them, and every entry is researched.

A note from Alex

Russell Ash was my late father. I worked with him as editor and picture researcher on It Just Slipped Out, and I'm proud to keep his books in front of new readers.

Want It Just Slipped Out back in print?

Russell's encyclopaedia of double entendres (Headline, 2010, which I worked on as editor and picture researcher) is out of print. If you'd buy a new edition, or have a favourite slip of your own, tell me. Every message helps make the case for a reissue.
